# SANGA — Story Bible

The script, the hidden truth behind it, and the one mechanic that ties it together. Every
line the player reads in the new build comes from this file. If a line in `story/*.json`
disagrees with this file, this file wins.

Lines are in natural, modern Tagalog, the way people in Manila actually talk: short sentences,
contractions (`'yan`, `'di ba`, `n'yo`), a little English where a Manileño would use it
(*rider*, *delivery*, *operasyon*, *joke lang*). No deep or textbook Filipino. Ending cards
and objectives stay in English, as they are now, because they also label the timelines
chart.

---

## 1. Logline

**"You can't undo a death. You can only remember it."**

Three ordinary days in Manila: a rider buying a toy for a kid, a priest hearing confessions on
a Saturday, a girl from the province waiting for a job. Every death the player sees becomes an
**Alaala** (a memory) that the game never forgets. Each Alaala opens one new choice in another
life. Only a player who remembers everything can reach the one run where nobody dies, and
find out who has been confessing to them all along.

---

## 2. The hidden truth (never shown all at once)

The player learns this in pieces. Nobody in the game ever says all of it.

| Who | What they seem | What they are |
|---|---|---|
| **Father Eli** | The kind, tired parish priest everyone trusts. | Breaks the seal of confession. What he hears in the booth he passes to Batista, so the people who know too much end up dead in "operations". He keeps Mercy locked in an apartment near the church. The girl before her did not leave it alive. |
| **Batista** | Eli's loud, loyal high-school friend, a police officer. | Runs the killings in the area and calls them *nanlaban*. Eli knows every one of them from Batista's own confession, and uses it. Batista returns anyone who calls the police for help back to Eli. In exchange, Eli gives him names. They called themselves *Eli-tista* in high school. "Ikaw ang kamay, ako ang dasal." |
| **Gloria** | The sweet market vendor who calls everyone *anak*. | Brings girls from the province with promises of work and sells them. Mercy is her niece. She also tips the police whenever someone buys a realistic toy gun from her stall: a "nanlaban" with a gun pays a reward. |
| **Kulas** | A tired addict trying to quit. | Was paid to carry a suitcase to an apartment a month ago. Something inside it moved. He is the only witness, and Eli knows it the moment Kulas confesses. |
| **Mercy** | A shy girl from Samar looking for a job so she can send money home (*padala*). | The next girl. She meets Eli in the church in the afternoon. That night she wakes up in the room. |
| **Peter** | A food delivery rider. | The rider who answers Mercy's order. |
| **Gwen** | Peter's girlfriend. Sharp, protective. | Pregnant, and hasn't told Peter yet. |
| **Ben** | Gwen's little brother. Wants a toy gun "'yung mukhang totoo". | Innocent. The wish that starts everything. |

**The frame (the last twist):** every run after the first (all but the Main Timeline) begins
with an unseen voice in a dark confessional:
*"Basbasan n'yo po ako, Padre, dahil nagkasala ako."* The player assumes it's a stranger, or
the story itself. Only in the true ending does it turn out to be **Father Eli**, confessing,
and the one he is confessing to is **the player**. The player has heard every confession in
the game and remembered all of them. That is what the Alaala are.

---

## 3. The deception, layer by layer

**Story time:** Kumpisal is the past, Padala the present, Tokhang the future. The Main
Timeline plays them as **future, past, present** (Tokhang, Kumpisal, Padala), so each story
recasts the one before it, and only its closing card puts them in order.

What the player believes after each stage, if they start with New Game (which always begins
with the Main Timeline):

1. **Run 1, Tokhang:** a slice-of-life story that turns tragic: a toy gun, a police
   operation, a mistake. Feels like a story *about* tokhang.
2. **Run 1, Kumpisal:** the player *is* Father Eli, kind and wise. The confessions feel like
   small human sins. A gunshot outside. Eli prays: *"Panginoon... patawarin Mo ako."* It reads
   as grief.
3. **Run 1, Padala:** Mercy, locked in a room. The police poster brings help, then Batista
   says *"Oh pare, ikaw pala."* At the door stands Father Eli. **The player has been playing
   the monster.** Every kind line from Kumpisal turns over in their head.
4. **Replays:** with each Alaala, scenes gain lines the player never heard before (see rule
   R4). The voice shouting "Pigilan n'yo 'yan!" gets a name. Gloria makes a phone call. Eli
   makes a phone call. Nothing was an accident.
5. **True ending:** Eli confesses for real. The prologue voice was his. He was talking to you.

### Double-meaning lines (first read vs. second read)

| Line | First read | After the reveal |
|---|---|---|

| Eli to Mercy: *"Sasamahan kita."* | Kindness. | He is taking her. |
| Eli to Batista: *"Ikaw ang kamay, ako ang dasal."* | High-school banter. | The division of labour. |
| Eli to Gwen: *"Si Peter... saan siya madalas mag-deliver?"* | Small talk. | He wants to know the rider. |

| Gloria: *"Ako na'ng magbabalot."* | Gift wrapping. | She packs girls into suitcases. |
| Mercy: *"Basta makapagpadala ako kay Nanay."* | A girl who wants to send money home. | She is the *padala*. |

## 4. The Alaala mechanic (final rules)

**Writing choices:** a choice never hints at what it leads to. Both options read as things the
character would plausibly say, and none sounds like the safe or the dangerous one. Every
choice fits on one line. Stage directions inside a line are Tagalog, between asterisks:
*Tumawa*, *Mahina, sa cellphone*.

- **R1.** Three kinds of choices: **branch** (existing: Tokhang phone call, Padala poster /
  flyer / key, Run / Ride Jeep), **Alaala** (new, need a memory, change the ending),
  **ordinary** (new, change only the lines right after them).
- **R2.** Alaala come only from deaths. Each death ending awards one Alaala; several deaths
  can award the same one.
- **R3.** Each Alaala unlocks exactly one Alaala choice, in one fixed scene.
- **R4.** An Alaala can also add **revelation lines** to scenes the player has already seen
  (no choice, just more truth). This is how replays peel the story open.
- **R5.** Alaala belong to the save slot and are never lost. Flags and choices reset each
  run.
- **R6.** No timers, no meters, no hidden stats, no stealth. The player cannot fail a choice.
- **R7.** No new places, no new characters.
- **R8.** An Alaala choice shows a faint **✦** before its text. Gaining an Alaala shows one
  line on the end-of-run screen: **"Hindi mo na ito makakalimutan."** and the Alaala's name.

### The five Alaala

| # | Name | Memory text (shown when gained) | From (deaths) | Unlocks (Alaala choice) | Revelation lines it adds (R4) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ang Laruang Baril** | *Isang laruan. Isang tawag. Isang bala.* | Peter shot, Gwen shot (Tokhang), Trade | Tokhang, choosing the gun | Tokhang: Gloria's phone call after the sale |
| 2 | **Ang Pagtakbo ni Kulas** | *Tumakbo siya palabas ng simbahan. Hindi na siya nakarating sa kanto.* | Kulas shot (Tokhang), Kulas shot (Kumpisal) | Kumpisal, Kulas' confession | Kumpisal: Kulas mentions the suitcase |
| 3 | **Si Father Eli** | *Ang taong nakikinig sa lahat ng kasalanan... may sarili palang itinatago.* | Mercy dies (Padala, police poster) | Kumpisal, Eli's last prayer | "???" becomes "Batista" in every "Pigilan n'yo 'yan!"; Eli's phone call before the Kumpisal gunshot |
| 4 | **Ang Baril ni Batista** | *Sa dilim, lahat ng tumatakbo ay suspek.* | Mercy shot while running (Padala) | Padala, the escape | — |
| 5 | **Ang Rider** | *Dumating siya para tumulong. Mag-isa.* | Peter stabbed (Padala, food delivery) | Padala, the call to the rider | — |

**Where each Alaala choice appears** (only where it can change something):

| Alaala choice | Appears in |
|---|---|
| 1, the water gun | Tokhang, every timeline |
| 2, "Sasamahan kita" | Kumpisal, where Kulas dies after the confessions: Main timeline, and Padala timeline (food chain) |

**True ending, "Walang Namatay":** in one **Main Timeline** run, take Alaala choices 1, 2 and
3. A first run that ends at the police poster already gives all three (Peter's death gives 1,
Kulas' death in Kumpisal gives 2, Mercy's death gives 3), so a careful player can reach the
true ending on their second run. A player who escapes with the key on run 1 needs one more
run to learn who is behind the door.

## 5. Voice guide

| Character | How they talk | Never |
|---|---|---|
| **Father Eli** | Always a priest, in every line and every choice: calm, warm, unhurried, calls people *anak*, speaks of God, prayer and forgiveness. Short, quotable sentences. Never raises his voice. The only exception is in secret (his phone calls, the door with Batista), where he says *pare*: that slip is the reveal. | Sermons longer than two lines. Slang in public. |
| **Peter** | Pagod pero pabiro. Taglish of a rider: *delivery, traffic, joke lang*. Calls Gwen *mahal*. | Self-pity. |
| **Gwen** | Mabilis magsalita, matapang, makulit kay Ben. Soft only when scared. | Being helpless. |
| **Ben** | Bata: simple, excited, one idea at a time. | Long sentences. |
| **Gloria** | Sweet tindera voice: *anak, suki, naku*. Laughs things off. | Saying anything directly. |
| **Batista** | Malakas, kanto-cop, *pare* every other line, laughs too loud. Hard when alone. | Formal Tagalog. |
| **Kulas** | Paos, pagod. Very short sentences, trails off with "...". | Jokes. |
| **Mercy** | Probinsyana, magalang (*po, opo*), soft, apologises too much. Grows firmer in Padala. | Swearing. |

## 6. Prologue (new, before every run except the Main Timeline)

The Main Timeline starts cold in the market, with no hint of what comes after it.

Black screen. The sound of a confessional's small door sliding open. Text only, no name plate.

> **(Isang boses):** Basbasan n'yo po ako, Padre, dahil nagkasala ako.
> **(Isang boses):** Matagal na po mula nung huli akong nangumpisal.
> **(Isang boses):** Hindi ko po alam kung saan magsisimula.

Last line depends on where the run starts:

| Run starts with | Last line |

| Kumpisal | **(Isang boses):** Siguro... sa simbahan. |
| Padala | **(Isang boses):** Siguro... sa kwarto. |

Then the story's title card, as now.

## 12. Presentation: 2D with 2.5D staging (decided)

The game stays **2D**. It gains depth through **2.5D staging**, not a 3D rebuild.

**Why not 3D:** a 3D rebuild means new models, rigs, lighting and a new art style, and it
would replace the hand-painted 90s anime look that sets SANGA apart. Judges score the
concept, the story and how finished it feels. A polished 2D game with a strong mechanic beats
a rough 3D one.

**What 2.5D adds (in this order):**

1. **Layered places.** Each of the four places is split into three depth layers
   (background, middle, foreground). The camera drifts slowly; nearer layers move more, so
   the room has real depth. The foreground layer already exists in the market (the blurred
   motorcycle); the rest follows that example.
2. **Living cutscenes.** Every cutscene picture gets a slow push-in, and the key reveals
   (Eli at the door, the trade, the visiting booth) are split into two or three layers for
   parallax.
3. **Breathing characters.** A subtle breathing and sway shader on standing characters and
   dialogue portraits.
4. **Light.** Light pools and moving shadows (window light in the apartment, candles in the
   church), building on the existing prop grounding.
